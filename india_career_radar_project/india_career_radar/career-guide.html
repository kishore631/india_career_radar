Your career guide

Practical steps to prepare for applications, interviews, and your first role.

01. Build a focused resume

Keep your resume clear, accurate, and tailored to the role. Highlight projects, internships, measurable results, relevant coursework, and skills you can demonstrate.

  • Use concise bullet points and action verbs.
  • Include working links to projects or portfolios.
  • Proofread contact details and dates.
  • Never claim skills or experience you do not have.

02. Prepare for interviews

Practice explaining your projects, problem-solving process, and decisions. For technical roles, revise fundamentals and solve problems consistently.

  • Prepare a 60-second introduction.
  • Use real examples to explain your contributions.
  • Research the company and role.
  • Prepare thoughtful questions for the interviewer.

03. Search strategically

Use role-specific keywords and track applications so follow-ups are easy to manage.

  • Set alerts on official company career pages.
  • Keep a tracker for role, date, link, and status.
  • Apply early when you meet the requirements.
  • Customize your resume for relevant openings.

04. Stay safe while applying

Fraudulent job offers may imitate real companies. Verify the domain and recruiter identity before sharing sensitive information.

  • Do not pay a fee to get hired.
  • Check the job on the official employer website.
  • Be cautious with requests for OTPs, passwords, or bank PINs.
  • Do not submit sensitive documents to unverified contacts.
