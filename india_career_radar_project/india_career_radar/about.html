ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

Helping you find your next step.

India Career Radar is a job portal starter project designed to make job discovery and career preparation simple and accessible.

Our purpose

We aim to organize job opportunities, internships, fresher hiring updates, and career guidance in one easy-to-browse place. The project is designed for job seekers exploring opportunities across India.

What you can explore

  • Searchable job listings with role and location filters.
  • Job detail pages with responsibilities and qualification examples.
  • Career preparation and salary literacy resources.
  • Links to official employer career pages where available.

Important transparency note

This starter website currently uses sample data. Listings, dates, descriptions, and any displayed information must be checked against the employer's official website. The site is not affiliated with the companies mentioned unless you separately establish such an agreement.

Before publishing, add a process for checking job listings, removing expired vacancies, protecting user information, and reporting suspicious posts.
