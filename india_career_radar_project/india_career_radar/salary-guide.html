UNDERSTAND YOUR OFFER

Salary and offer guide

Learn what to review when comparing job offers. Actual pay varies by company, role, location, skills, and experience.

What does CTC mean?

Cost to Company (CTC) is the employer's annual cost for employing you. It may include fixed pay, variable pay, employer contributions, insurance, and benefits. CTC is not necessarily the amount deposited in your bank account each month.

Understand these salary terms

  • Fixed pay: the regular salary components specified in your offer.
  • Variable pay: compensation that depends on performance or other conditions.
  • Gross salary: earnings before employee-side deductions.
  • Take-home pay: the amount paid after applicable deductions.
  • Probation: an initial employment period with terms set by your offer and applicable law.
  • Benefits: items such as insurance or retirement contributions; read the offer to understand their value and conditions.

Before accepting an offer

  • Read the full compensation annexure and probation terms.
  • Ask for a written monthly salary breakup and estimated deductions.
  • Check work location, shift, notice period, bond, and variable-pay conditions.
  • Verify the recruiter and offer through official company channels.
  • Do not pay money to obtain an offer or release an appointment letter.
